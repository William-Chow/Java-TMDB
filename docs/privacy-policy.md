# Java TMDB Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what data the Java TMDB Android app (package `com.movie.tmdb.app`, "the app"), published on Google Play by Will.ii.am ("we", "us"), collects, why, and the choices you have.

## Summary

- We don't run any servers for the app, and we don't receive or store your personal information.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK in the app collects device and usage data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
- Movie information comes from The Movie Database (TMDB). Your browsing and search requests are sent to TMDB to fetch results.
- Your favorites and the movie lists cached for offline use stay on your device.

## Advertising: Google AdMob

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects the following and shares it with Google:

- your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
- device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
- your interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, ad taps and video views;
- diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK.

Google uses this data as described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Movie data: TMDB

Movie lists, details, cast, trailers and search results come from the [TMDB API](https://www.themoviedb.org). When you browse a list, open a movie or search, the app sends that request, including any search terms you type, to TMDB to get the results. We don't store your search terms. TMDB's handling of these requests is described in the [TMDB privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Data stored on your device

Movies you save as favorites, and the movie lists cached so the app works offline, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Your choices

- **Consent in Europe.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from **Privacy settings** at the top of the app's main screen. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.
- **Advertising ID.** On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device's settings, which stops it from being used for personalized ads.
- **Uninstalling** the app stops all collection described in this policy.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Internet** and **network state**: to load movie data and ads, and to tell you when you're offline.
- **Advertising ID** (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

## Data retention and deletion

We don't hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google's policies, linked above.

## Security

All data the app sends, to Google and to TMDB, travels over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
